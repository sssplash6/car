# Central Asian Review — product context

Read together with DESIGN.md (visual system) and AGENTS.md (engineering rules).

## What this is

A public academic review: research, analysis and essays on the politics,
economies and societies of Central Asia (Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan,
Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan). Authors upload PDFs, an editor reviews, abstracts are
public, the full PDF needs a free account. Published by Freshman Academy; the
review fronts its own brand.

## Register

**Brand** on every public surface (home, papers, issues, abstract pages, about):
the visitor's impression of scholarly seriousness IS the product, because the
site's job is to make the review worth citing and worth submitting to.
**Product** on working surfaces (submit, submissions, notifications, admin):
editors and authors visit these repeatedly, so they stay quiet and fast.

## Users

- **Readers** — researchers, students, analysts, journalists who arrive from
  search on an abstract. Most read one page and leave; the design has one
  chance to say "this publication is real and cared for".
- **Authors** — regional academics and graduate students deciding whether this
  venue is credible enough to submit to.
- **Editors** — a small Freshman Academy team living in /admin.

## Brand voice

Three words: **illuminated, woven, scholarly.** The physical object is a
hand-bound journal on warm paper, its cover an ikat cloth, its endpapers
Samarkand tile. Decoration is craft that frames the scholarship — never a
costume over it.

Named palette lane: *Timurid manuscript* — warm paper field, lapis and
turquoise tilework carrying identity, pomegranate and gilded ochre as
supporting threads. Committed strategy, not restrained-beige and not a
saturated drench.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI editorial": cool grey minimalism, one teal accent, rule-separated
  columns and nothing else. (This was the site's own v1; the rework exists
  because it read as institutional-anonymous.)
- Tourist-bazaar kitsch: ornament on every element, literal photo collages of
  yurts and flags. Ornament belongs at boundaries and beginnings.
- SaaS template energy: hero-metric blocks, icon-card grids, gradient text.
- National symbolism used carelessly: no flags, no state emblems, no motif
  captioned as belonging to a specific country. Geometry is shared heritage;
  attribution is politics.

## Strategic principles

1. **Abstracts carry all SEO weight** — public pages stay server-rendered,
   crawlable, fast. Decoration must cost near-zero: inline SVG ornament, no
   pattern image downloads, no layout-shifting entrances.
2. **Trust before delight.** A reader deciding whether to cite must never feel
   marketed to. Motion resolves and settles; nothing loops, nothing bounces.
3. **The review is bilingual in spirit** — the region is the subject, not a
   backdrop. Ornament derives from the region's geometric tradition
   (girih stars, ikat lozenges, the shanyrak roof-wheel) rather than from
   stock photography of it.
